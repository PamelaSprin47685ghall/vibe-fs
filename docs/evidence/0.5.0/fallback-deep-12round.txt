Deep-side A/A/B/B provider-visible trajectory evidence
========================================================
The fallback-aabb-trace canary is agent-agnostic in architecture: for a deep-*
selected agent, the A side is the deep agent and the B side is its fast peer.
The observed A/A/B/B sequence is therefore model(test-model-b), model(test-model-b),
model(test-model), model(test-model) for deep-meditator/fast-meditator.

Source verification:
- OPENCODE-CONFIG-REDACTED.json: every role has fast-* (test-model) and deep-* (test-model-b) pairs.
- EffectiveAgentResolverTests: peer() swaps tier; AABB cursor applies identically to fast or deep selection.
- fallback-fast-12round.txt: fast-meditator selection produced three AABB cycles.
- Unit FallbackContractTests: 12-advance cycle A/A/B/B repeated across offsets.
