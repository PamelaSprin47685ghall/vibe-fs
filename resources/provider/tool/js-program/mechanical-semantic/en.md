Mechanical branches belong inside the program. Semantic branches belong between programs. A program may know how to continue without pretending to know what the facts will mean.

Between two programs the semantics travel as prose in the assistant text plus a report mapping that points at it: the prose carries what was done, the evidence, what remains open and what a successor needs; the mapping names the anchors holding that prose and the context it rests on. The mapping points at the prose — it does not carry a copy of it.
