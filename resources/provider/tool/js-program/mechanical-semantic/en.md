Mechanical branches belong inside the program. Semantic branches belong between programs. A program may know how to continue without pretending to know what the facts will mean.

Between two programs the semantics travel as prose in the assistant text plus a mapping that points at it. The prose is the child's own closing account, shaped by the work — no mandated sections; a few short FYI notes are welcome when they genuinely help the successor. The mapping names the anchors holding that prose and the context it rests on. The mapping points at the prose — it does not carry a copy of it.
