Receive arrived consequences from people or terminals that have returned
or ended, and from nearer arrivals.

Use join when receiving an arrived consequence is useful now.
Wait only when a real dependency makes waiting useful — when every remaining
useful action depends on something not yet known.

join does not freeze the road until everyone returns.
It does not dump hidden machinery.
It does not replace horizon: orientation is a different act.

A successful return hands you the consequences that have arrived,
or tells you there is nothing away to receive.
