Continue a person already known in this mission by handing them a new charge.

resume does not call a new person. To create one, use fork with calling + name + charge.

The person is identified by name. resume reuses everything already bound to
that person: its office, persona, reasoning depth, and the full history of
its earlier work in this mission.

Entrust the new charge, relevant constraints, and the evidence or boundary
that matters. Do not prescribe the person's hidden tools.

Passing calling is rejected: choosing an office belongs to fork, and a
person's bound office is never re-chosen here.
