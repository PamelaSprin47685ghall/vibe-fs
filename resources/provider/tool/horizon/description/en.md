Orient to what remains at your horizon — who is still away, who has returned,
which terminals remain open, and each visible subagent's latest work record.

horizon is pull-only. Use it only when that orientation would change your next
action. Do not poll it: it does not watch, subscribe, or refresh in the background.

horizon does not receive arrived consequences; that is join.
It does not dump hidden machinery about how lives are kept.

A successful return is an ordinary-language snapshot: who is away, who has
returned, each visible subagent's latest work record (or that none is available
yet), which terminals remain open, or that nothing beyond immediate sight
presently asks for attention.
