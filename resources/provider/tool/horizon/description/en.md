Orient to what remains at your horizon — who is still away, who has returned,
which terminals remain open.

Use horizon when that orientation would change your next action.

horizon does not receive arrived consequences; that is join.
It does not dump hidden machinery about how lives are kept.

A successful return is a roster in ordinary language: who is away, who has
returned, which terminals remain open, or that nothing beyond immediate sight
presently asks for attention.
